# Faith's Birthday Bloom

Create a beautiful, romantic, and deeply personal birthday celebration website for my girlfriend. The design should feel warm, modern, elegant, and heartfelt.

### Aesthetic & Styling:

- Palette: Soft pastels, warm blush pinks, cream/ivory, with subtle rose gold accents.

- Typography: Elegant serif for headings (like Playfair Display or Cormorant Garamond) paired with a clean, readable sans-serif for body text.

- Vibe: Emotional, romantic, interactive, and polished.

- Animations: Smooth scroll, subtle fade-ins on scroll, and light interactive micro-animations (e.g., subtle heart floating/sparkle effects on button click).

### Structure & Page Sections:

1. Hero Section:

   - Floating confetti or gentle falling heart animation.

   - Large headline: "Happy Birthday, Faith Funmilayo ❤️"

   - Subtitle: "A digital love letter for my favorite person."

   - A smooth scroll-down button (e.g., "Begin the Journey ↓").

2. Interactive Love Letter Section:

   - An elegant "envelope" component that users click to open/unfold.

   - Inside: A beautifully formatted long-form letter with blockquotes for special highlight lines.

3. "Reasons Why I Love You" (Interactive Cards or Grid):

   - A grid or carousel of expandable/flip cards highlighting 6–8 distinct reasons (e.g., Her Kindness, Her Smile, Shared Adventures, Quiet Moments).

   - Card click reveals a short, touching note for each.

4. Memory Lane / Gallery Section:

   - A timeline or photo grid layout for favorite moments together.

   - Hover effects on photo placeholders with caption overlays.

5. "Reasons to Celebrate Today" / Wish List:

   - Interactive checklist or bucket list for our upcoming year together (e.g., "Trip to [Destination]", "Stargazing night", "Cooking pasta from scratch").

6. Footer:

   - A final sweet message: "Forever and always yours, [My Name]"

   - A hidden interactive button that triggers a big full-screen birthday confetti blast.

Include responsive mobile navigation, smooth scrolling anchor links between sections, and placeholder data so I can easily swap in my actual photos, text, and dates.
